## Execution feasibility and provenance

The repository documents sudo-free Linux-bridge emulation and a real-OVS path requiring privileges; the real 5G deployment requires Docker, Open5GS and UERANSIM. Existing manifests establish successful execution. No new experiment was run for this paper: the checked-in confirmatory datasets cover the stated comparisons, and rerunning the full pipeline is documented as approximately 6.5 hours. The 5G radio is simulated and all radio resource scheduling claims are unavailable.

There are additional exploratory/historical artifacts under `results/evidence/qos_eval_v1`–`v3`, `baseline_v4`, `qos_eval_v2`, and older pipeline timestamps. They are superseded for primary estimates by the predeclared `final_eval_v5` results, but are retained to disclose iteration and defects discovered/fixed. Raw `results/5g/` filenames encode many per-run outputs; the validation report and `v32_runs.csv` are the aggregate index.
