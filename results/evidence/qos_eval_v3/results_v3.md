### Earlier evaluation v2 (seeds 5–9) and what it exposed

contended-regime v2 (seeds 5-9), after v1 fixes. Defects found and fixed (with regression tests) before the next run on fresh seeds:

* elastic (TCP) demand-estimate death spiral: a starved TCP slice sends less, the per-direction estimate follows it down and the anti-starvation guard (20 % of that estimate) collapses with it (general SLA 0.05 at school@50Mbps, 0.10 at school@1.5x)
* not changed: agriculture degraded in emergencies (0.78 vs 1.00 in disaster): declared context priority healthcare 10 : agriculture 1 gives burst room to healthcare (+0.09 to +0.17); reported as a policy trade-off, not tuned
